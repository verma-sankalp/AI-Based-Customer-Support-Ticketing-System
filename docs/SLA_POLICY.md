# SLA Policy

## Priority Levels
- Low: response within 24 hours, resolution within 72 hours.
- Medium: response within 8 hours, resolution within 24 hours.
- High: response within 2 hours, resolution within 8 hours.
- Urgent: response within 30 minutes, resolution within 2 hours.

## Breach Rules
- A ticket is near breach if 80 percent of its SLA time is used.
- A ticket is breached if the due time has passed.
- Breach alerts should notify agents and supervisors.
